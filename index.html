About

Hello! I am a student at University of California, Berkeley. I am majoring in mechanical engineering student with an aerospace concentration. Welcome to my portfolio!

I became fascinated with aerospace engineering when a Boeing 747 flew over my head. I couldn’t understand how something so massive could stay in the air and that curiosity drives me to learn more about the physics and engineering behind aircraft. Today I’m building on that curiosity by being a part of the landing gear design team on Cal Aero SAE where I design and CAD a modular landing gear test rig to analyze various loads on a landing gear. I hope to work and gain experience in the aerospace industry as I learn the theory behind aircrafts.
